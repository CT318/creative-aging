# 背面圖（QR）棚拍提示詞

先自己拍一張「從後方看」的實物照（底座背面，最高那件背面貼好 QR，構圖盡量跟主圖同高度、同角度，光線不用管）。
注意：Gemini 會重畫前景，QR 很容易被生壞（掃不到）。因此 **QR 不要讓 Gemini 生**，流程是：
1. 先用下面的提示詞把環境換成棚拍；
2. 拿到結果後，QR 那塊我用真 QR 貼回去（掃得到）。

```
Edit this photo. Keep the product exactly as it is: the hexagonal white paper bases and the paper-cut city parts standing on them, seen from behind, including the QR code on the back of the tallest part. Do not redraw, restyle, move, resize or add anything to the products; do not alter any black-and-white QR pattern, edges, colors or shapes. Keep the same camera angle and framing.

Change ONLY the environment into a clean professional product-photography studio, matching the earlier front-view shot: a seamless warm light-gray backdrop that fades softly into a smooth matte light-gray tabletop (slightly warm beige-gray), no clutter, no horizon line. Soft diffused light from a large softbox at the upper left with gentle fill from the right, soft natural contact shadows under the bases, no glow, no color cast on the products, whole product sharp.

No added props, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 第 1 步的「手機對著 QR」圖（選配）
若簡報要有手拿手機掃的畫面，另用這則（純生成，不需原圖）：

```
Photorealistic studio product photography on a seamless warm light-gray backdrop, soft diffused softbox light from the upper left, calm warm mood. Seen from behind at a slight elevated angle: a small paper-cut world-city model with a white hexagonal paper base, watercolor-illustrated paper parts standing in staggered layers. The tallest part at the back shows its blank white back side with a clean black-and-white square QR code (about 20 mm). An elderly Taiwanese hand holds a smartphone above it; the phone screen shows the camera view with the QR framed by four corner brackets and a soft green highlight. The front parts are slightly out of focus. Natural hand anatomy with five fingers. No text other than the QR, no logos, no watermark.
```
