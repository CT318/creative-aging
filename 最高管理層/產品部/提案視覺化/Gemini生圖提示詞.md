# 外出小盒｜Gemini 生圖提示詞（A、B 各 4 張）

用法：每張圖先貼「共用風格段」，再貼該張的「畫面段」。一次只生一張；想改細節就在最後加一句修正。
我沒有生圖功能，這份是給 Gemini 用的。生出來的圖放回本資料夾，檔名照下面編號（A-1、A-2…），簡報會用同樣編號放圖。
提示詞用英文，因為 Gemini 對結構與動作描述的英文比較穩；每張附中文說明。

## 共用風格段（每張都貼）

```
Photorealistic product concept rendering, soft diffused studio-quality daylight, warm off-white and light grey background, shallow depth of field, clean modern Scandinavian-Japanese stationery design, matte materials. Product: an ultra-thin pocket sketchbook ("tezhou", approx. 105 x 148 mm, portrait, about the size of a small passport notebook) with a soft, flexible cover made of matte sand-beige PU leather. Thin magnetic modules in muted blue-grey and warm grey. Natural proportions, no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark. Calm, elderly-friendly, approachable mood.
```

材質固定：軟質沙色 PU 封面、霧面。人物固定：長輩（約 65 歲，短灰髮，亞洲臉孔，休閒淺色外套），手要自然，五指數量正確。

## 提案 A　flip 手札

構造要點（生圖時要保持一致）：直式小本子；封面是軟質、與本體背面同一片，**上緣是折線**；翻開方式是**封面由下往上翻過上緣，貼到本體背面（手背那側）**；封面夾層內有**兩支筆的筆袋，開口在上緣朝天**；封面外側有**磁吸溝槽**吸水彩模組；水彩模組取下後**轉 90° 橫放，掛在本體底緣**。

### A-1　構造總覽（爆炸圖）
中文：本體闔上，旁邊爆炸展開：軟質封面（夾層有兩支筆的筆袋）、紙塊本體、直式薄水彩模組（6 色固體水彩＋小海綿＋滑蓋）。

```
[共用風格段]
Exploded product view on a seamless light background, three-quarter top-down angle. Center: a closed portrait pocket sketchbook with a soft sand-beige PU cover and a shallow rectangular magnetic groove on the cover. Floating above-right: a thin vertical watercolor module (about 45 x 100 mm) with a frosted translucent sliding lid, six pans of solid watercolor in red, yellow, green, blue, violet, brown, and a small grey sponge. Floating left: the soft cover shown from inside, with a flat sleeve pouch holding two slim pens, the pouch opening at the top edge. Thin dotted guide lines connect the parts. Studio product render, crisp, minimal.
```

### A-2　單手由下往上翻
中文：長輩單手握住闔上的手札，拇指頂著封面下緣，封面正在向上翻起（翻到一半）。

```
[共用風格段]
Close-up of an elderly person's left hand holding a closed portrait pocket sketchbook upright, the thumb pressing the lower edge of the soft cover, the cover being flipped upward from the bottom edge, rotating over the TOP edge of the book (the fold line is at the top), caught mid-motion at about 120 degrees. Inside of the cover is visible with a flat pen sleeve. Background softly blurred city plaza. Natural hand anatomy, five fingers.
```

### A-3　翻到背面，筆袋朝天，抽筆速寫
中文：封面已完整翻到本體背面（在手背那側），紙面完整露出；兩支筆露頭朝上，另一手抽筆畫。

```
[共用風格段]
An elderly person standing in a quiet street, holding the pocket sketchbook in one hand. The soft cover is fully folded over the top edge and lies flat behind the book, against the back of the hand. The full blank page is exposed in front. Two slim pens stand in a pouch at the top edge pointing to the sky, their caps just visible about 6 mm above the book. The other hand pulls one pen out and begins a quick line sketch of a plant in a pot. Side-lit, shallow depth of field, photorealistic.
```

### A-4　水彩：模組橫放掛在底部
中文：水彩模組取下、轉 90° 變橫式，掛在本體底緣；封面已翻到背面；一手握本體，另一手用水筆畫。

```
[共用風格段]
A watercolor setup. The same pocket sketchbook held in one hand, cover folded behind. A thin watercolor module (six solid pans, small sponge, frosted sliding lid pushed open) has been rotated 90 degrees to landscape and hangs flush at the BOTTOM edge of the book, locked by magnets, extending directly below the page like a built-in palette. A water brush stands in the top pouch, one pen pulled out in the other hand painting a green leaf on the page. Sitting on a park bench, soft morning light.
```

## 提案 B　左右翻小本

構造要點：小筆記本（比 A 大一圈，約 120 x 160 mm），**左側裝訂、左右翻開**，構造像精裝書：兩片薄硬板＋中間軟質折線（不用機械鉸鏈）；翻平後**兩板邊緣磁吸，成為一個硬平面**；封面外側有**磁吸溝槽**吸薄型模組；翻開後**平面左右邊緣各有一組磁吸座**；紙墊是**一整墊單張紙，可在平面上滑動**，模組在哪一側，紙墊就往另一側移。

### B-1　構造總覽（爆炸圖）
中文：闔上的筆記本，爆炸出：封面（外側有溝槽）、兩片薄板＋軟質折線、可滑動的紙墊（背面鐵片）、薄型模組（速寫版＋水彩版）。

```
[共用風格段]
Exploded product view, three-quarter top-down. Product is a slightly larger pocket notebook (about 120 x 160 mm), bound on the left, opening like a book. Layers float apart: soft sand-beige PU cover with a shallow rectangular magnetic groove on the outside; two thin rigid boards joined by a soft flexible fold line like a hardcover book; a single-sheet paper pad with a thin steel plate on its back; two thin vertical modules (about 50 x 150 mm), one holding two slim pens in channels, one with six watercolor pans, a sponge, and a water brush. Small magnet dots visible along the left and right outer edges of the opened boards. Clean studio render.
```

### B-2　翻開成硬平面，模組放到側邊
中文：筆記本放桌上，翻開成一個平整硬平面；模組正從封面搬到右側磁吸座，紙墊已滑向左側。

```
[共用風格段]
Top-down 45-degree view of a wooden cafe table. A notebook lying fully open, both halves forming one perfectly flat rigid surface with a thin soft fold line in the middle. The paper pad has slid to the left half. A slim vertical module is being lowered by a hand onto a magnetic docking strip along the RIGHT outer edge, sitting flush with the page surface. Right-handed layout. Warm window light, coffee cup blurred at the side.
```

### B-3　速寫使用
中文：模組（兩支筆）在慣用手一側，抽出一支筆，畫在左邊的紙墊上。

```
[共用風格段]
Over-the-shoulder view of an elderly person sketching at a table. The opened notebook forms a flat rigid surface. Paper pad on the left, a slim vertical pen module docked on the right edge flush with the surface, one pen pulled out and being used to draw a plant in a pot on the pad. The drawing hand rests naturally on the open flat area between pad and module. Realistic hands, five fingers.
```

### B-4　水彩使用，紙墊換到另一側
中文：水彩模組在右側，紙墊在左側；左手慣用版可以另貼一張鏡像圖。

```
[共用風格段]
Close-up of a watercolor session on a table. Opened notebook as a flat rigid surface, paper pad on the left half with a loose watercolor of green leaves, thin watercolor module docked flush on the right edge with six pans, sponge and water brush visible, frosted lid slid open. An elderly hand holds the water brush. Soft daylight, shallow depth of field. Photorealistic.
```

## 生圖小技巧
- 結構常會畫錯（例如封面翻到錯邊）。出錯時在原提示詞後加一句具體修正，例如：「The cover must fold over the TOP edge, not the side edge.」
- 手的指頭數常錯，生成後挑最自然的一張。
- 圖 1（爆炸圖）先生，其他圖用它當參考圖上傳，外型會比較一致。
